# Brief — audit phase 2b: the paper, claim by claim

- **Agent:** `leakage-auditor` (fable, high). Read-only and adversarial. Write only into `.claude/briefs/iteration-5/audit-2b-findings.md` and `.claude/briefs/iteration-5/audit-2b-scripts/`.
- **Abdou's rule of 2026-11-19 (`CLAUDE.md`).** Propose a bolder check only if it would significantly improve the paper. This brief overrides any older line in your agent file. The depth of the audit does not change.

## What exists

- **The paper:**
  - `paper/main.tex`, `paper/refs.bib`;
  - `paper/numbers.tex`, generated by `paper/make_numbers.py` from `docs/RESULTS.md`, `docs/PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/HYPOTHESES.md`, the runs' JSON and audit 2a's tables.
- **Its brief:** `.claude/briefs/iteration-5/paper.md`, with its claims, its "Never claim" list, its "Must contain" list and its dated changes. Two of those changes concern citations:
  - a source is cited only for what the parts read support;
  - a method paper is cited for its definition alone (`reading-methods.md`).
- **The records:**
  - `docs/RESULTS.md`: the rows of 2026-11-19 are the iteration-5 results, written after audit 2a;
  - `docs/PROTOCOL.md`: the change-log lines of 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19, the last one "after audit phase 2a";
  - `docs/HYPOTHESES.md`;
  - `audit-2a-findings.md`, whose "## Sentences" section says what the paper may state as it is and what it must word differently.
- **The reading notes:** `reading-papers.md`, `reading-credits.md`, `reading-terms.md` and `reading-methods.md`.

## Do

1. **Every number.**
   - It must be a macro of `numbers.tex`. Flag any digit typed in the prose that is a measured quantity; identifiers and dates are fine.
   - Run `cd python && uv run --no-sync python ../paper/make_numbers.py` into a scratch copy and check that it regenerates `numbers.tex` byte for byte.
   - For a sample of at least 40 macros, weighted to the abstract, §5 and §8.4, check the source each one names against the file.
2. **Every claim.**
   - It must match a `docs/RESULTS.md` row, a run's JSON, or the registered text.
   - Check the "Never claim" list sentence by sentence: OLS is attention; signed weights; an exact ridge state; the idea of a two-run test; OLS beats softmax; any profit; any result from the vault or the validation year.
   - Check the wording audit 2a requires: H15's cell named as one run against two, all leaks, convention G; the ×3 and ÷3 comparisons beside it; the not-constant interval; P's reversal; the date-dependent sentence with the labels unchanged across subsets; Minor 4's TWO-RUN wording; Minor 5's case-study wording; N1, N2, N3, N7 and N8.
3. **Every citation.**
   - It must rest on a part its reading note records as read, and its `%` comment must name that part.
   - A method citation stands for the method's definition only.
   - Flag any statement about a source that goes beyond what the note records.
   - Flag bibliography fields marked UNCONFIRMED that the paper relies on.
4. **The "Must contain" list:**
   - the AI use, reported as arXiv requires;
   - the data credit and the licence, as pending;
   - the reproducibility section: the commits, the run folders, the rerun identity, the two code digests;
   - the vault's exposure, stated;
   - every item not run, reported as not run.
5. **The overall framing.**
   - The contribution is the measurement, and it must survive our detector losing. Flag any sentence that reads as a win for FI or for OLS Prime.
   - Flag any novelty claim not narrowed against the prior art in the reading notes.
6. **It compiles:** with MiKTeX, `pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode --disable-installer`, then `bibtex`, in a scratch copy outside `paper/`. Report undefined references and citations.

## Never

- Run anything that reads real bars or a registered line.
- Open `runs/vault/` or the validation year.
- Run `git stash`, `checkout`, `reset`, `add` or `commit`.
- Download or install anything.

## Report

- **The verdict:** GO, GO with fixes, or NO-GO. Rate each finding Blocker, Major, Minor or Nit, with the line of `main.tex`.
- **The fixes:** for each finding, the exact replacement text, or the macro to add.
