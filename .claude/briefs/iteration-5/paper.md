# Brief — the paper draft

- **Author:** the orchestrator. `leakage-auditor` reads every claim (audit phase 2). `teacher` does not write it.
- **Where:** `paper/` at the repository root, LaTeX, in English: `paper/main.tex`, `paper/refs.bib`, `paper/figures/` (figures generated by a script from the runs' JSON, never by hand).
- **Motto:** ambitious in what we measured, never in what we claim.

## Working title

"Measuring a look-ahead detector: future invariance against its rivals on a generated population of temporal leaks, with a pre-registered case study of OLS attention on crypto bars". The title is the orchestrator's draft; Abdou decides.

## Claims, in the order of their strength

1. **The measurement.** Catch rates with Wilson intervals, honest-twin false positives and the classification of misses, for every detector on the same fresh population. Future invariance is presented as an instance of Fonseca's two-run class, not as an invention.
2. **The pass rule's size and power**, measured (Report of 2026-10-30).
3. **Matrix**, worded as an absence in the literature read, never as a priority: an exact ridge readout on any window of a raw, never-compacted log, decomposable per period and per feature. Credit regression cubes, Log-Linear Attention, MesaNet, Goulet Coulombe 2504.09663 and 2412.13076, and Garnelo & Czarnecki.
4. **The case study.** H2, H3, H10–H12 (Report n°2) and H4, H5, H13 (Report n°3) exactly as `docs/RESULTS.md` records them: H4 as non-inferiority, superiority as a description; H5 not shown, with its refit cadences; H13 without a verdict. The skill split and the factor control as exploratory.

## Never claim

OLS is attention; signed weights; an exact ridge state; the idea of a two-run test; OLS beats softmax; any profit. No result from the vault or the validation year: both are sealed, and the exposure of BTCUSDT 1d vault rows is stated (found on 2026-10-28 by Audit A of iteration 2, not in iteration 1: corrected 2026-11-19, audit 2b N10). The validation year is not behind the code seal: each pipeline drops its bars before any computation (audit 2b m4).
